# Fewer style rules, reports that make sense

September 19, 2026. Three bounded follow-up tests in the portraits worktree. **No application code, model defaults or deployment changed.**

## Recommendation

**Remove the hard paragraph-start quota next.** Keep the preference for readable, human-centered prose in the writing brief, and judge the complete reading for sense, useful development and fidelity to its sources. A count of sentence openings should not trigger a paid rewrite or prevent delivery by itself.

Tony's editorial standard is now explicit: fewer formal style rules; no fixed mold for a report. Preserve prose that works. Edit a passage when a concrete problem harms the reading, and be able to explain the benefit. Reading grade, repeated phrases and stylistic counts can help investigation; they are not independent reasons to rewrite an otherwise useful report.

Checking first and reserving editing for specific defects remains the broader cost direction. The new evidence is stronger for removing one arbitrary style gate than for declaring the entire acceptance system solved.

## The useful subtraction

The current rule rejects a draft when more than 15% of its paragraphs start with selected astrology terms. Both challenged drafts had four such openings among twenty paragraphs. They passed the remaining structure checks, and the unchanged Opus meaning/source check accepted both complete readings.

A free local sensitivity check exposed the problem with the threshold. In each draft, rearranging one sentence opening made the entire report pass. The factual relationship and point of the sentence stayed the same; the count simply moved from four flagged openings to three. Exact before/after examples remain in the private receipt. This demonstrates a change in the count, not better prose.

Those technical openings are understandable in their surrounding paragraphs. That does not make every sentence excellent or establish owner preference. It does show why this particular count is a poor reason to discard a whole reading. Preserve the meaning/source check; it can still reject actual incoherence, pervasive exposition or a consequential source error.

## Keep source checking proportionate

The new Personal issue is a distinction between a planet ruling one house and being located in another. The draft describes this as governing both areas, a stronger claim than the packet. The broader thematic connection remains supported. This is a bounded source-precision finding, not a fabricated life event. Its materiality is agent-assessed, not owner-approved.

The relation-format experiment kept the checker and prose fixed and made those source roles explicit. It did not change this acceptance. The earlier Life Chapter error was still detected for the right reason. This is useful negative evidence: clearer field names alone did not earn another runtime layer. Do not equate a small uncorrected wording issue with a lost central insight, or silently tighten the prose standard until every report needs editing.

[QASemConsistency](https://aclanthology.org/2026.tacl-1.6/) and [D-FActScore](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.160/) motivated the relationship test. Their role and limits are recorded in the [research review](LLM_WRITING_RESEARCH_2026_09_19.md) and private experiment decision. Their relevance did not guarantee a local win.

## Another cost revealed, without changing it yet

Two first attempts exceeded the private plan's 1,500-character schema maximum, so the complete visible prose was generated again. In the Personal failure, the second attempt then hit the paragraph quota. The schema limit and the style quota are separate causes.

This supports examining how much private planning text the writer needs. It does not authorize ignoring malformed output or loosening every schema. First remove the demonstrated stylistic hard gate. Consider a shorter planning representation separately, with source trace and reader quality preserved.
