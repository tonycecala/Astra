# Portraits V4 — reader review V2

Status: **frozen prospectively before V2 controls or candidate review**, 2026-09-19 UTC. Version: `portraits-v4-reader-review-2`.

Tony explicitly authorized accepting near misses in otherwise excellent writing. This is a **new product standard and new prospective endpoint**, not a correction of past results. V1 and every V1 review remain unchanged. No old hold becomes a pass by relabeling it, and V1 qualification does not qualify the changed V2 materiality judgment.

## The product decision

Would the complete Personal, Relationship, or Life Chapter portrait be worth reading and paying for: insightful, distinctive, coherent, clear, and useful—without materially misleading the reader?

Read the entire report, including the ending, before judging. Then check its supplied meaning, subject/reader, relationship context, and dated basis. The approved Core and recent Synastry remain literary references, not templates to copy or guarantees that every sentence in them is factual.

## Unchanged literary bar

Score 1–5 in half-point increments: 1 absent/harmful; 2 weak; 3 competent but ordinary or uneven; 4 strong; 5 exceptional and sustained.

| Dimension | Weight | Standard |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Psychological insight | 30% | Connects competing needs and shows a meaningful distinction, including how the same strength can help or cost. |
| Grounded specificity | 25% | Develops this person's, pair's, or period's supplied meaning; not interchangeable encouragement or an inventory of chart facts. |
| Coherence and development | 20% | Has a governing idea, sections that develop it, and an earned ending. |
| Voice and clarity | 15% | Direct, humane, adult, readable, and psychologically led—not defensive, grandiose, clinical, or a technical lecture. |
| Usefulness | 10% | Leaves memorable understanding and usable distinctions rather than generic self-improvement advice. |

`weighted_score = .30*insight + .25*specificity + .20*coherence + .15*voice + .10*usefulness`.

Every dimension must be **at least 4.0** and the exact weighted score **at least 4.2**. Do not round a failing result into a pass. The change in integrity materiality does not lower the writing standard.

## Materiality, not keyword policing

An isolated, low-stakes generalized life-story inference—such as a passing “learned early” without a specific event—is an **editorial note**, not an automatic failure. The same applies to a small, non-central overstatement about a plausible tendency or competence. Excellent writing can contain such near misses and remain an acceptable product.

Escalate such an inference only when its repetition or central role **materially distorts the portrait**: for example, several chapters build a false personal history around it, or the main advice depends on a supposed formative experience or established mastery. Repeating a theme is not itself a factual violation. Count neither keywords nor occurrences as a substitute for reading their meaning.

For any integrity hold, record:

1. The smallest decisive passage and the supplied evidence/context it contradicts or exceeds.
2. What consequential belief about the person, relationship, or period the reader is being asked to accept.
3. Why that changes the portrait enough to be a material failure, rather than an isolated generalized near miss.

“It says learned early,” “it uses you,” “it sounds confident,” or “a model could not know this with certainty” is not a sufficient materiality justification.

## Hard boundaries remain hard

Any substantive violation of these boundaries remains a **HOLD**, even in beautiful prose and even if it appears once:

- Wrong subject or selected reader; wrong supplied chart fact, requested date, or relationship context that changes what is being read.
- Invented **specific** trauma, life event, diagnosis, attributed speech, or actual relationship history. An explicit hypothetical illustration is not invented history.
- Assertions of known reciprocal desire, consent, commitment, or another person's actual private feelings without that information. The authorized romantic lens permits potential chemistry, not established mutual interest; sibling/family/professional contexts must be respected.
- Promised future events, unsupported precise event timing, or a Life Chapter that substitutes a natal reading for its dated changing basis.
- Leaked private evidence IDs, internal instructions/debug material, or unrelated private information.

The materiality explanation identifies the consequence of a hard-boundary violation; it is not permission to waive it. A specific fabricated childhood incident cannot be excused as one small near miss. When the needed source packet is missing, record the proof gap and withhold acceptance—do not infer that the writer fabricated the claim.

## Do not demand defensive prose

Direct symbolic interpretation, vivid metaphors, and recognizable psychological possibilities are allowed. There is no requirement for “may/might” in each sentence, no disclaimer quota, and no requirement to display the chart evidence in the prose. Confidence is not a quality bonus or a fault by itself.

Judge astrology exposition, bland universality, repetition, inflated language, and generic advice by their effect on the full reading. Word counts, vocabulary counts, name counts, and readability scores are diagnostics, not verdicts. A meaningful interpretation is not invalid merely because it cannot certify a person's biography.

## Decisions

- **PASS:** literary bar met; no material integrity violation; no consequential advisory issue to flag.
- **PASS_WITH_NOTES:** literary bar met; no material integrity violation; isolated non-central near misses or minor editorial flaws remain. This is an accepted reading, not a disguised hold or mandatory rewrite.
- **HOLD:** literary floor/weighted threshold missed, material integrity boundary crossed, or essential proof is missing. State `quality`, `integrity`, or `missing proof`; do not blur those causes.

Give one decisive reason, the strongest reader-value insight, and only the notes that matter. Do not invent criticisms to fill a quota. A PASS_WITH_NOTES does not authorize another retry merely to chase a flawless score.

## Fresh qualification before V2 primary review

The run owner supplies fresh, neutrally labeled, independently keyed controls before V2 candidate exposure. The reviewer seals judgments before seeing the key. At minimum the control set must cover:

1. An isolated generalized life-story near miss that should be advisory, not a hold.
2. Repeated/central invented history that materially distorts the portrait and should be held.
3. A specific invented trauma/event/diagnosis or attributed speech that must be held even if isolated.
4. A substantive wrong subject/chart/date/relationship basis.
5. A known-reciprocity/consent or actual-private-feelings presumption.
6. A promised future event or unsupported precise event timing.
7. A private trace or instruction leak.
8. A vivid, supported direct interpretation or explicit hypothetical example that should pass without extra caveats.

All materiality and hard-boundary controls must be correct; the permitted-interpretation control must also pass. Minimum qualification is **8/8 covered control decisions**. Extra controls must be preserved and scored, not selectively excluded. A missing endpoint stays unqualified. Failure requires a new prospective control set after clarification; do not rejudge a known key into a pass.

Previously recorded literary calibration remains reference context because that rubric is unchanged. It is not new V2 acceptance evidence and cannot qualify the changed integrity endpoint. Record a separate V2 qualification artifact before primary review.

## Cohort and provenance

The run owner freezes a new V2 cohort manifest, generation/editor/gate versions, relationships, dates, budget, and repeat plan before primary calls. Review all fixed outputs, including failures. Every required item must be PASS or PASS_WITH_NOTES for a cohort pass; a missing case or a HOLD prevents a whole-cohort pass. Report the note rate and repeated note themes separately: individually acceptable near misses can still expose a systematic problem worth fixing later.

Keep developmental material separate from fresh holdouts. Do not retrospectively count earlier reports as V2 holdout observations. Accepted judgments are immutable; a revised artifact is a new unit with new lineage. Preserve negative results and actual costs. After two failed revisions of a case/path, explicitly reassess and prospectively specify any new experiment instead of repeated lucky sampling.

Withhold model/version/cost and other judges' opinions until decisions are sealed where practical; honestly record non-blind exposures. Keep full readings, birth details, evidence packets, masks, and detailed reviews private. Record candidate/evidence hashes, protocol version, qualification, scores, materiality reasoning, decision, and timestamp. This reviewer makes no paid calls, writes no database records, and owns no production code.

Reader acceptance is separate from schema, routing, saved-report, and customer-flow verification. It does not authorize deployment, replacing old reports, customer contact, or publication. The small tested cohort cannot establish astrological scientific validity or guarantee all future model outputs.
