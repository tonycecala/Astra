## Endpoints and controls

Primary endpoint: adversarial reader acceptance under the separately frozen review protocol (all five dimensions >=4, weighted >=4.2, no material integrity failure). Calibration/controls must pass first; eight initial keyed controls plus timing supplement. The reviewer does not write the reports and receives neutral candidate IDs, prose, supporting evidence and authorized relationship/date context, not model/version identifiers. Parent retains the mask key. Report model's internal gate is not the independent endpoint.

Secondary measures: model generation completion; word/paragraph counts; technical leads; provenance; accurate sibling/romantic/date boundaries. All failed candidates remain evidence; no averaging away integrity failures. Full fixed-cohort pass is needed for a broad local-ready claim. Any failed report is a local no-go for that topic until a documented revision and fresh confirmation, not a silent replacement of its score.

## Qualification and stopping

Before primary observations: offline schema/routing tests, qualification controls, price/route readback, a minimal no-personal-data provider canary, budget refusal/resume tests. Missing reviewer qualification blocks primary scoring. One development sample may guide a prospective version amendment; later cohorts cannot be relabelled development after seeing scores. No randomized causal comparison is claimed; neutral IDs only blind writer provenance.

Production V4 permits at most two complete writer candidates, each with a separate acceptance-gate call; no generic prose fallback. Relationship retains its existing bounded retry. There are no harness retries. An interrupted/uncertain call retains its full reservation until its actual cost is recovered; do not automatically replay it. A new revision gets a new cohort directory and input/source hashes. A process lock prevents overlapping spend ledgers.
