# Decision Card: TC–CA playful prose and editor value

**Status:** frozen before acquisition, September 19, 2026
**Owner:** Tony
**Privacy class:** confidential named relationship interpretation; same owner-authorized OpenRouter → Amazon Bedrock private route, ZDR, data collection denied, no fallback. No new provider/disclosure class.

## Decision

Does a paid editor improve this one TC–CA reading enough to justify adding an editing stage? If yes, can Haiku do it as well as Opus? This is everyday owner-calibration learning, not a population claim or production qualification.

## Test

- **Unit:** one fresh TC–CA romantic-potential draft from the frozen September 19 direct evidence packet. No approved prose supplied to the writer.
- **Change:** lighter, playful, everyday-language writer contract (`synastry-playful-private-v1`). Target grade 7–8; 1000–1300 words advisory. Six topics retained; remove paragraph-level rhetorical formula and obligatory long ending in this private experiment.
- **Comparison:** unchanged new Sonnet 4.6 draft versus Haiku 4.5 and Opus 4.8 edits of that identical draft. Editors receive byte-identical prompts and evidence; reasoning disabled for all calls, 6000 output-token bounds. This compares those configurations, not the earlier medium-reasoning substantive editor.
- **Primary measure:** supervising reader judges complete versions with model/role labels hidden: ease, warmth/playfulness, felt impact, and preservation of sharp insights. An editor earns its cost only if at least two dimensions improve with concrete passages, none materially worsen, and no material evidence/history/consent distortion is introduced. If edits tie, choose the cheaper; if no editor clears that bar, choose no editor.
- **Secondary:** body-only readability, word count, actual provider cost. These never substitute for reading or become literary scores.
- **Smallest valid sample:** one draft, two independent editor outputs, no paid model judge. Randomized A/B/C labels conceal attribution until written judgment. Supervising reader knows the brief and may recognize textual ancestry; masking is partial, not independent scientific blinding. Tony is the final taste authority.
- **Stop:** three calls, maximum $1 additional and original cumulative $30 cap. No automatic retry; preserve any failure and remaining reservation when cost is unknown. Never regenerate accepted output. No manual polish of primary candidates.

## What could fool us

1. One report and one draw per model cannot establish reliability or a general winner.
2. The same supervising agent authored the brief; editorial judgment is not an independent calibrated evaluation.
3. Shorter prose may score easier while losing insight; compare the complete reading and source support, not the number alone.

## Evidence and decision

Three calls completed, no retries, total $0.096330 (writer $0.025002; Haiku edit $0.009513; Opus edit $0.061815). Ledger now $4.395551. Private artifacts: `.astra-exports/portraits-v4/tc-ca-playful-v1/`; `FINAL_REPORT.md` contains the mini-report and owner-reading link; `MASKED_REVIEW.md` preserves pre-reveal judgment. Initial progress labels were re-randomized before reading any candidate prose. Exact editor prompt equality and raw requests/responses are preserved.

Opus produced the best sentence-level ease and warmth; Haiku made mostly marginal trims. Body grades were 5.1 (writer), 5.3 (Haiku), 3.8 (Opus), advisory only. All inherited unsupported assertions that another person's withdrawal was a test rather than rejection. Both editors missed that issue; it was not introduced by the editing intervention. The initial masked conclusion conflated inherited errors with the preregistered prohibition on introduced errors; the final mini-report explicitly corrects that interpretation without overwriting the original judgment. Opus showed local stylistic value, not end-to-end factual clearance. One case does not justify a general model winner or a mandatory editing stage.

The final owner copy is a disclosed parent-authored revision, not an unedited model success. No runtime promotion. Recommendation: writer-first playful voice, no automatic cheap edit, optional Opus style pass when demonstrably needed; preserve a separate integrity review. Owner taste calibration precedes any broader production change.
