# Astra Semantic Synthesis v1

Status: shipped on `alpha`

Version: `1.0.0`

Prompt identifier: `astra-report-writer-2026-07-semantic-synthesis-v1`

## What v1 establishes

Astra Semantic Synthesis v1 enriches the existing report-section signal-card path without introducing a graph service, new contracts, database changes, routes, packages, or UI.

- Curated section cards may supply a hypothesis, counterweight, and claim boundary.
- Evidence is assigned by astrological role rather than named aspects or one person's chart.
- Canonical Identity is generated once and reused across Core and Deep.
- Enriched Core uses sectioned generation while ordinary Core retains its existing fallback.
- Deep chapters receive evidence for distinct jobs:
  - Relationships: relational dynamics.
  - Work: allocation and contribution.
  - Drive: proportion and force.
  - Gifts: bounded capacities.
  - Blind Spots: observation versus interpretation.
  - Growth: self-updating.
  - Integration: values and decision criteria.
- Cross-chapter semantic repetition is evaluated as repeated mechanisms and conclusions, not keyword overlap.
- Strained or ending relationship disclosure is conditional on safety.
- Gifts describes capacities as possibilities rather than established biography or categorical behavior.

The Tony relationship-context controls passed deterministic, semantic, repetition, and human-review gates.

A second CLI control used saved, resolved charts for Felicia Weiss, Cheyenne Autumn, and Marissa Yahil:

- Three Core and three Deep reports completed.
- Role-based evidence ownership generalized beyond Tony.
- Gifts calibration held across all three Deep reports.

## Continuous Refactoring backlog

These are improvement targets, not blockers for continued internal testing:

- Canonical Identity should avoid invented biography and claims about how others experience the reader.
- Work should avoid inferred reputation, income behavior, social impact, or claims about how others use the reader's abilities.
- Blind Spots should not imply privileged access to another person's needs, hidden state, or emotional undercurrents.
- Emotions and Blind Spots should further differentiate pause/check/wait conclusions.

Future changes should continue to improve prompt inputs and evidence boundaries instead of adding corrective output checks.
