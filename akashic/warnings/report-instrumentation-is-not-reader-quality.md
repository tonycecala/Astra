# Report instrumentation is not reader quality

## What Happened
In imaginative report experiments, word counts, name-frequency quotas, literal tone keywords, and conservative blind-model preferences are review instruments, not automatic reader failures. Preserve human product judgment, use explicit fatal categories, allow plus or minus 10 percent on numeric targets, and green-light prose unless more than two fatal categories remain. Fabricated direct quotes, concrete invented biography, wrong evidence, technical leakage, malformed traces, protagonist erasure, and severe semantic misrepresentation remain fatal.

## Why It Happened

The experiment treated measurable prompt compliance and a blind model's conservative preference as if they were the product verdict. A clear, simple portrait that succeeded for its actual reader was labeled failed because it was shorter than requested, used implicit rather than literal tone words, and hid the evidence more successfully than the control. The rubric optimized auditability over the intended reading experience.

## How To Avoid It

1. Separate fatal integrity errors from editorial review notes before generation.
2. Apply ±10% to numeric targets and report the result without turning every miss into rejection.
3. Green-light the portrait unless more than two fatal-error categories remain.
4. Treat direct reader judgment as the product verdict; use blind models and counts as evidence, not authority.
5. Keep wrong evidence, technical leakage, malformed traces, fabricated quotes/biography, protagonist erasure, and severe semantic misrepresentation fatal.
