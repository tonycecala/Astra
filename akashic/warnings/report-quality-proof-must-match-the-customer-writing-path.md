# Report quality proof must match the customer writing path

## What Happened
A model-written report can still read like fallback prose when a later aspect-by-aspect instruction overrides its psychological brief, or selected chart evidence loses its actual values. A passing schema-1 bakeoff and synthetic prose fixtures did not qualify the schema-2 customer path. Validate the exact production entry and saved basis schema with complete selected evidence, full independent readings, materiality-aware review, and preserved failures. For progressed readings, bind each aspect participant to its natal or progressed identity; do not borrow the counterpart placement. Routine artifact checks belong in deterministic code and do not replace whole-reading judgment. Procedure: akashic/playbooks/astra-verification-tiers.md; evidence: docs/evaluations/ASTRA_PORTRAITS_V4_ENGINEERING_REVIEW.md.

## Why It Happened
Mechanics tests used synthetic prose and an earlier request schema. Their success was incorrectly treated as proof of the customer's writing path. Conflicting writing instructions and lossy selected-evidence serialization remained outside that proof. Later full-reading review also exposed a natal/progressed participant substitution that schema and link checks could not detect.

## How To Avoid It
Use the saved production request schema and actual dispatch entry, preserve complete selected values and participant roles, and independently judge full readings against the authorized product standard. Keep internal acceptance gates separate from the independent endpoint. Allow isolated low-stakes editorial near misses while holding materially wrong evidence or specific invented history. Use deterministic inspection for mechanical checks, retain failed observations, and identify corrected outputs as new units. Do not infer customer-path quality from another report family or synthetic fixture.
