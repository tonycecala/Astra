# Curated chart-signal packets cannot prove absent contacts

## What Happened
A strongest-signal packet is affirmative evidence, not a complete chart inventory. Report prompts and evaluators must reject claims that an unlisted reciprocal contact does not exist or that one person contributes more solely because their contacts were not selected.

## Why It Happened

A report writer received the strongest four contacts for each legacy synastry
evidence job. The prose correctly described those contacts but then treated the
selection as the entire chart, using omitted reciprocal contacts to support a
one-sided labor conclusion.

## How To Avoid It

Label selected evidence as curated and non-exhaustive. Allow positive
interpretation of supplied contacts, but forbid absence claims and comparative
contribution rankings based on unlisted evidence. If the product needs a
complete inventory claim, provide the complete inventory instead of a ranked
subset.
